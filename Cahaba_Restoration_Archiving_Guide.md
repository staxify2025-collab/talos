# Cahaba Restoration: Leap-to-Dash Archival Agent Guide

## Overview

This guide explains how to use the Talos Autonomous Agent to extract historical job data from Leap CRM and seamlessly archive it into Dash CRM.

## Step 1: System Login

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Log into your **Leap CRM** account.
3. Open a new tab and log into your **Dash CRM** account.

## Step 2: Prepare Dash CRM for the Archive

1. In your Dash CRM tab, create a **single, empty job** specifically to hold your archived files.
   _(Recommended Name: "Leap Storage" or "2023 Leap Archives")_
2. Keep this Dash CRM tab open.

## Step 3: Point the Agent at Your Data

1. In a third browser tab, open the **Staxify Admin Dashboard**.
2. Go back to your Leap CRM tab and find the historical job(s) you wish to archive.
3. **Copy the URL link** from the top of your browser for that specific job.
4. Return to the Staxify Dashboard and locate the **"Start AI Extraction"** section.
5. Ensure the **"Web URL Extraction"** button is selected.
6. **Paste your Leap URL(s)** into the text box.

**_Pro-Tip: Batch Processing_**
_You can process multiple jobs at once! Simply copy up to 50 urls from Leap and paste them all into the text box in the dashboard. Just ensure each URL is on its own line or separated by a comma._

## Step 4: Let the Agent Work

1. Click the blue **"Start Autonomous Agent"** button.
2. That's it! The Staxify Vision AI will autonomously navigate to the provided Leap jobs, extract all relevant Customer, Financial, and Date information, compile it into a comprehensive "Archive PDF Summary," and automatically drop everything into your designated Dash CRM storage folder.
